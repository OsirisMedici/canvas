# Community guidelines

Help make Canvas V5 feedback useful and welcoming.

- Treat people respectfully, including when you disagree. Critique the idea or behavior, not the person.
- Keep reports constructive and focused on Canvas V5. Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information, private work, credentials, or private conversations.
- Use original or appropriately permitted examples and attachments.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and other activity in this repository. The repository maintainer, @OsirisMedici, may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation when needed.

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, flag the relevant conversation to the maintainer without repeating harmful or private content. For sensitive abuse reports, use GitHub's reporting controls where available and avoid posting details publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
